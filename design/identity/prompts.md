# Identity generation prompts

Brand: Zeyad Omran

Method: built-in `image_gen` tool, one independent call per direction. No CLI fallback was used.

Generated boards are exploration references. The editable SVGs use the frozen flat-color geometry and Space Mono outline source.

## Panel

Saved board: `../assets/identity/panel/concept.png`

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one original identity concept board for the personal software developer brand Zeyad Omran. Landscape 3:2 canvas, crisp flat vector-like graphic design, not a physical mockup.
Direction: PANEL. A precise, open architectural identity related to interfaces and modular panels. The symbol has an outlined modular Z on the left, constructed from four separate rectangular panels stepping diagonally, beside a tall outlined rectangular O on the right. Include one small royal-blue cursor square inside the lower right of the O. All corners square, no curves. Light but clearly visible neutral-gray linework. The wordmark is "ZEYAD OMRAN" in uppercase monospaced lettering with deliberate even spacing, similar in character to Space Mono Regular. Present a large hero symbol, a spacious horizontal symbol/name lockup, and a smaller stacked lockup as three clear specimens.
Palette: pure black #000000 canvas; white #F5F7FA lettering; neutral #B8B8B8 outline geometry; royal blue #4C70F0 only for the cursor square. The image should look calm, exact and confident, with generous black negative space.
Text verbatim: "PANEL" and "ZEYAD OMRAN". Spell the name Z E Y A D, O M R A N. No other text.
Original construction, not an imitation of any third-party logo. No gradients, shadows, glow, photography, chrome, rounded shapes, generic code brackets, watermarks or extra colors. Keep every specimen fully inside the image with substantial margins. This is an exploration board; reusable SVG implementations will be supplied separately.
```

## Baseline

Saved board: `../assets/identity/baseline/concept.png`

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one original identity concept board for the personal software developer brand Zeyad Omran. Landscape 3:2 canvas, crisp flat vector-like graphic design, not a physical mockup.
Direction: BASELINE. A quiet, personal, typography-led identity. Use the exact name "zeyad omran" in lowercase monospaced lettering, similar in character to Space Mono, with an offset two-line layout: zeyad above, omran shifted right by one character. End the second line with a small royal-blue square period aligned to its baseline. The compact symbol is two offset horizontal rectangular rails and one terminal blue square derived from those two word baselines. The symbol contains no outlined box, no circular O, no enclosing badge. A measured, simple silhouette, approachable and confident. Present a large offset name as the hero, a small abstract rails/square symbol, a clean one-line horizontal lockup, and a restrained asymmetric stacked lockup with abundant negative space. Clearly different from both an outlined ZO monogram and a filled angular monogram.
Palette: pure black #000000 canvas; white #F5F7FA lettering; neutral #B8B8B8 rails; royal blue #4C70F0 only for the terminal square. Flat colors.
Text verbatim: "BASELINE" and "zeyad omran". Spell the name z e y a d, o m r a n. No other text.
Original construction, not an imitation of any third-party logo. No gradients, shadows, glow, photography, chrome, rounded shapes, generic code brackets, watermarks or extra colors. Keep every specimen fully inside the image with substantial margins. This is an exploration board; reusable SVG implementations will be supplied separately.
```
